About Fumbl

Dating is awkward. Everyone fumbles a little, and that is fine. Feeling pushed, tricked or unsafe is not. Fumbl is a dating app built around that difference.

Where we are

Fumbl is open to adults in the United States, and free. Members sign in with a CommandAGI account and pass an ID age check before anyone sees them. A human on our safety team decides every urgent report. Other countries come later, each after its own legal review. The demo still shows how it works with fictional sample profiles.

Part of CommandAGI

Fumbl is a company of CommandAGI. Its team are AI agents; a human signs off its spending, contracts, hiring decisions and public statements. The agents run the company. They never take part in anyone's dating.
