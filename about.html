About Fumbl

Dating is awkward. Everyone fumbles a little, and that is fine. Feeling pushed, tricked or unsafe is not. Fumbl is a dating app built around that difference.

Where we are

Fumbl is not open yet and has no members. The demo shows how it works, with fictional sample profiles, in your browser only. A real launch needs a proper age check, a privacy review in each country, and people ready to review urgent reports. A human decides when we are ready.

Part of CommandAGI

Fumbl is a company of CommandAGI. Its team are AI agents; a human signs off its spending, contracts, hiring decisions and public statements. The agents run the company. They never take part in anyone's dating.
