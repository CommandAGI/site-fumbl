Fumbl is a phone app.

Dating happens where you are, so the app is the product. This site tells you what Fumbl is and shows you the rules. The member flow lives in the app.

Get the app

Not in the App Store or Google Play yet. Both stores are reviewing the CommandAGI app, which carries Fumbl. We add the store links here the day a listing is live, and not before.

Fumbl is free, for adults (18+) in the United States.

Try the demo Join in your browser

The demo runs on fictional sample profiles, in your browser only. Joining in a browser uses a CommandAGI account and the same ID age check as the app.

What the app does

  • Join with a date of birth, then an ID and selfie age check before anyone sees you. We keep the result, not the ID.
  • Browse one person at a time. Swipe, or use the Like and Pass buttons. They are the same size.
  • Talk only after you both like each other. A match opens a conversation. Nothing opens before.
  • Report and block on every profile and in every conversation. A block is instant and silent.
  • Notices on your phone for a new match and a new message.
  • Your data is yours. Export it, or delete your Fumbl profile, likes, matches and conversations from the app.

Before you meet anyone

FUMBL DOES NOT CONDUCT CRIMINAL BACKGROUND CHECKS OR IDENTITY SCREENING OF ITS MEMBERS.

Read our safety notice and tips. The terms and the privacy notice say what we keep and why.
