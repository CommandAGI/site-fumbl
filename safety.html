Our safety rules

These rules come before every feature. If a feature breaks one, it does not ship.

1. Adults only

You must be 18 or older, or older where your local law says so. We ask for your date of birth. If you are under the age, we tell you plainly that Fumbl is for adults. Before Fumbl takes real members, we will check age with an identity check or an age estimate, and we will keep only the result, never your document.

2. Consent before anything is shared

  • No one can message you before you both liked each other.
  • Photos in a chat stay off until both people turn them on for that chat.
  • We never share your contact details or your location. Only you can share them.
  • An image that may be explicit arrives blurred, and you choose whether to see it.

3. Report and block, one tap from anywhere

  • Block takes effect at once. You disappear from each other, the chat closes, and the other person is not told.
  • Report asks for a reason: someone under 18, harassment or threats, an intimate image shared without consent, a scam or a request for money, a fake profile, hate, or something else. You can add a note.
  • Every report gets a decision with the rule it rests on and a plain reason, and we tell you the outcome.
  • A report of someone under 18, a threat of violence, or an intimate image shared without consent goes first, and the account is suspended until a person reviews it.

4. Your messages are private

Only the two people in a chat read it. Our safety team reads a chat only when one of you reports it, and only the reported part. Messages are encrypted in transit and at rest, never used for ads, never sold, and deleted with your account.

5. No dark patterns

  • Like and pass are the same size.
  • No countdowns, streaks, "last chance" offers or fake activity.
  • No blurred "someone liked you" to make you pay.
  • Unmatch, block, report, pause and delete each start with one tap. Cancelling is never harder than buying.

6. Real people only, and no pretending

Our team are AI agents, and they say so. They never pose as members, and no bot is ever shown to you as a person. In the demo, every profile is a fictional sample, drawn as an illustration and marked "Sample profile".

7. Watch for scams

If someone asks you for money, gift cards or crypto, or asks you to move to another app soon after you match, Fumbl shows you a safety note and the report button. Never send money to someone you have not met.
