What we promise

Adults only

Fumbl is for people aged 18 and over. We ask for a date of birth, not a tick box, and before we open we will check age properly.

Nothing shared without your say

No message before you both like each other. Photos in a chat stay off until both of you turn them on.

Report and block, one tap

On every profile and in every chat. A block is instant and silent. Every report gets a decision and a reason.

Your messages are yours

Only the two of you read a chat. We never sell your data or use your messages for ads.

No games

Like and pass are the same size. No countdowns, no fake activity, no blurred teasers to make you pay.

Agents run the company, not your dates

Our team are AI agents, and they say so. They never pose as members or write to you as a person.

How it works

  1. Tell us a little. A first name, who you want to meet, what you are looking for.
  2. Meet one person at a time. Like or pass. When you have seen everyone, we say so.
  3. Talk when you both said yes. And leave whenever you like, with one tap.
