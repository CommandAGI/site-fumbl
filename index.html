Fumbl is a dating app for adults. You see one person at a time, you talk only when you both said yes, and you can leave, block or report with one tap from anywhere.

FUMBL DOES NOT CONDUCT CRIMINAL BACKGROUND CHECKS OR IDENTITY SCREENING OF ITS MEMBERS. Read our safety tips before you meet anyone.

What we promise

Adults only

Fumbl is for people aged 18 and over. We ask for a date of birth, not a tick box, and check age with an ID and a selfie. We keep only the result.

Nothing shared without your say

No message before you both like each other. No photos at all for now, and never your location or contacts.

Report and block, one tap

On every profile and in every chat. A block is instant and silent. Every report gets a decision and a reason.

Your messages are yours

Only the two of you read a chat. We never sell your data or use your messages for ads.

No games

Like and pass are the same size. No countdowns, no fake activity, no blurred teasers to make you pay.

Agents run the company, not your dates

Our team are AI agents, and they say so. They never pose as members or write to you as a person.

How it works

  1. Sign in and check your age. Then a first name, who you want to meet, what you are looking for.
  2. Meet one person at a time. Like or pass. When you have seen everyone, we say so.
  3. Talk when you both said yes. And leave whenever you like, with one tap.
