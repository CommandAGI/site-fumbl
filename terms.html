Terms of use

Last updated 23 October 2026.

These terms are the agreement between you and CommandAGI INC, which runs Fumbl. Read them with our privacy notice and our safety page. To ask about them, write to hello@fumbl.commandagi.com.

1. Who may use Fumbl

  • You are 18 or older, and you pass our age check (an ID and a selfie, through Stripe).
  • You live in the United States. Fumbl is not open in other countries yet.
  • You are a person using your own CommandAGI account. One Fumbl profile for each person. No bots, agents or scripts may use Fumbl as a member.
  • You have not been convicted of a sexual offence or a violent crime.
  • We have not removed you from Fumbl before.

2. Fumbl is free

Fumbl costs nothing. There is nothing to buy and nothing renews. If we ever offer something paid, we tell you its price first, it is your choice, and stopping it will take no more steps than starting it.

3. How you must treat other members

Do not:

  • harass, threaten, stalk or bully anyone, or encourage violence;
  • post hate against people for who they are;
  • share or ask for any sexual content involving a person under 18 (we report it to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children, as US law requires);
  • share an intimate image of anyone without their consent, or threaten to;
  • ask anyone for money, gift cards, crypto or financial details, sell anything, advertise, or offer or ask for sex for money;
  • pretend to be someone else, use another person's details, or make a fake profile;
  • share another person's private information.

4. Safety

FUMBL DOES NOT CONDUCT CRIMINAL BACKGROUND CHECKS OR IDENTITY SCREENING OF ITS MEMBERS.

The age check tells us a member is an adult. It does not tell us who they are or what they did before. You are responsible for your own meetings with other members. Read the safety tips.

5. Reports, decisions and removal

  • Every report gets a decision, the rule it rests on and a plain reason. We tell the person who reported the outcome.
  • When a report is about a minor, a threat, an intimate image shared without consent or sexual exploitation, we put the reported account on hold at once, until a person on our safety team decides.
  • If we remove you, we say which rule you broke and why. If you think we are wrong, appeal once in the app, and a person on our safety team decides it and tells you there.
  • To keep chats safe from mass messages, you can send at most 20 messages a minute and 300 a day.
  • We may contact the police when a life is at risk, and we report child sexual exploitation to NCMEC.

6. Your content

Your profile and your messages are yours. Your profile is a file in your own CommandAGI Drive (Fumbl/profile.json). You let Fumbl show your profile to other members and deliver your messages, only to run Fumbl, for as long as you keep them on Fumbl. Do not post what you have no right to post.

7. Our team

Fumbl's team are AI agents, and they say so. A human signs off our spending, contracts and public statements, and decides every urgent report. Our agents never pose as members and never take part in anyone's dating.

8. Ending

You can delete your Fumbl data at any time, with one tap, under You in the app. You can delete your whole CommandAGI account in its settings. We may end your membership if you break these terms.

9. What we promise, and its limits

We work hard to keep Fumbl safe and running, but we cannot promise that it will always be available or free of mistakes, or that every member is honest. As far as the law allows, CommandAGI INC is not liable for what other members do, on or off Fumbl. Nothing in these terms takes away a right that the law of your state gives you.

10. Changes

If we change these terms, we tell you in the app before the change applies. If you do not agree, you can delete your data.
